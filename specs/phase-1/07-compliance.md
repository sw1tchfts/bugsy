# Spec 07: Compliance: credentials, records, notices, state rules

Status: done
Depends on: 06
Skills to load: pesticide-compliance, pest-domain

## Goal
The licensed manager can prove the business is legal without keeping a second set of books. Every completed pesticide visit becomes an application record with the fields the state requires, locked when the state says it must be complete and corrected only by a logged amendment. Every notice the state requires (post-application information, pre-application offers, resident notices, posting, registry advance notices) exists as a row with a due time, a permitted channel and proof. Notification registries are imported per state and matched to properties so dispatch's advance-notice block has teeth. Credentials and business licenses raise alerts before they lapse. An inspector gets records, notices and licenses for a date range in one file. All of it reads the state's rule pack; nothing in this spec names a state.

## Users and stories
- As a licensed manager, I open Compliance and see what needs me today: credentials and licenses lapsing within 60 and 30 days, records waiting to lock, notices past due, registry matches with no advance notice yet.
- As a licensed manager, I open any completed visit's application record, see every field the state requires with the crew and credentials that applied, and when I find an error I file an amendment with a reason; the original stays visible beside the correction.
- As a licensed manager, I see which notices the state required for a visit, when each was due, how it went out and the proof, and I record the proof myself when it went by paper, phone or a posted sign.
- As a licensed manager, I upload the state's notification registry, see which of our properties are on it, and know that dispatch cannot schedule a matching job until the advance notice is recorded.
- As a licensed manager, I export every record, amendment, notice and license for a date range in one file for a state inspection.
- As a dispatcher, when a visit is blocked by a registry match I record the advance notice from the visit page and schedule it.
- As an owner, I renew a branch's business license and the alert goes away; I see the roster of licensed employees per branch.
- As a technician, I see my own credential and its expiry in the app and nothing else here.
- As a customer (11, 16), the post-application information reaches me by the channel I consented to, and by paper when I did not.

## Scope
### In scope
- Tables: application_records, application_record_amendments, notices, notification_registries, compliance_tasks; `license_expires_on` on branches; rule pack keys for records, notices, posting, registries and business licenses typed in `lib/compliance/rules/index.ts`.
- Record generation from the visit at completion (spec 06's sync) and from a safety-net job; locking by the pack's completion rule; amendments; retention date; immutability enforced in the database.
- The notice engine: which notices a visit, agreement or property needs, when each is due and by which channel, as a pure function of the pack, the contacts' consent and the property; notice rows with proof; print views; proof recorded by hand for paper, mail, phone and posted signs; email and SMS rows left `due` for spec 11 to send, printable meanwhile.
- Registry import (CSV with header mapping), address matching into `properties.registry_matches`, re-matching when a property is saved, the advance-notice row, and the dispatch block lifting once it is sent.
- Credential and business license alerts at 60 and 30 days as compliance tasks; renewal tasks closed when the new expiry is saved; the licensed roster per branch.
- The compliance job (`POST /api/compliance/run`, `npm run compliance:run`): generate missing records, lock due records, create due notices and alert tasks. Idempotent; safe to run every hour.
- Inspector export: a zip of CSV files plus a printable binder for a date range and state, logged in the audit trail.
- Office pages under `/compliance`: home, credentials (exists, gains alerts), licenses, records, record detail with amendments, notices, notice detail and print view, registries, export.
- Seed: Lakeshore and Northwind each get a completed, fully captured visit from yesterday with a locked record, one amendment, a printed post-application notice, a registry with one matching property, a credential expiring in 45 days and a branch license on file.

### Out of scope
- Sending email and SMS and reading delivery receipts (11). Spec 07 creates the rows, renders the content and accepts proof; 11 drains `due` electronic notices and writes `sent_at` and proof.
- Inventory deductions from records (18). Record snapshots carry product amounts; 18 reads them.
- Platform management of rule packs and keeping records after a tenant leaves (13). Here records restrict tenant deletion so nothing is lost before 13's export exists.
- Device programs, audit binders for commercial sites (14); WDI and termite forms (15); customer-facing notice views in the portal (16).
- State-specific report formats and registry file formats: each comes with its state's pack (`report.format`, `registry.fileFormat`) when that state is researched. Until then the generic CSV bundle and the header-mapped CSV importer apply.
- Researching any state. No pack exists; conservative defaults apply and are named below.

## Business rules
1. Every completed visit whose job kind is a pesticide application (`isPesticideVisit`, spec 06) gets exactly one application record, created in the completion transaction and, failing that, by the job. Non-pesticide visits (inspection, trapping, exclusion) get no application record; their visit and captures are the service record. (pesticide-compliance: application records)
2. The record snapshot holds, from the visit at that moment: property address and state, service type and job kind, business date, arrival and departure instants (UTC) with the tenant's time zone, every product line (name, EPA number, amount and unit, carrier, site, location, target pests, label check, applied by), the crew with each member's role and credential (certification number, categories, expiry) as of that moment, areas treated, target pests, conditions, the pack version and `required_fields`, and `missing_fields`. Imported or otherwise incomplete visits produce a record with `complete = false` and a `record_incomplete` task; spec 06's gate means visits completed in the app are complete.
3. Locking: `lock_due_at` comes from `records.completeBy`; without a pack it is the end of the business date in the tenant's time zone (the conservative reading of "same day"). The job locks a due record by re-snapshotting the visit (captures added since count), stamping `locked_at`, `locked_by` and `retention_until`. A manager may lock earlier with `lockRecordNow`. A locked record never changes: the trigger refuses updates and deletes. (CLAUDE.md: records immutable once locked)
4. Amendments: only `compliance.write` (compliance manager, owner) may amend, with a reason; an amendment lists changed fields with their old and new values; the record view shows the locked snapshot and every amendment, and `applyAmendments` gives the current reading used by exports. Amendments are append only. (PRD spec 07 answer)
5. Retention: `retention_until` is the business date plus `records.retentionYears`; null when the pack has no value, meaning keep until a pack says otherwise. Nothing in the product deletes a record, an amendment or a notice; the tenant foreign keys restrict deletion so spec 13's export must run first.
6. Which notices exist is decided by `requiredNotices` from the pack, never by the UI: `notice.postApplication` creates a post-application notice to the account's primary contact due `withinDays` after departure; `notice.preApplicationOffer` creates an offer on a continuing agreement at signing, due `beforeFirstApplicationDays` before the first application; `notice.resident` creates one notice per resident contact (and one `posting_sign` for the building when a multi-unit property has no resident contacts) for the property types it names; `sign.restrictedEntry` creates a `posting_sign` notice when any product line has a restricted entry interval, with proof being a `posting` photo from the stop; `registry.advanceNoticeHours` creates a `registry_advance` notice per registry match when the pack's `registry.triggers` cover the work (spec 05 rule 5), due that many hours before the visit's scheduled start (mail: `registry.mailLeadBusinessDays` business days before). Each row records its `rule_key` and `required = true`.
7. Without a pack the defaults are: a post-application notice with `required = false`, `rule_key = default` and no legal due time (it is still the customer's summary, spec 11 sends it); resident notices for `apartment_building` and any property with `unit_count > 1` (`required = false`, conservative: notify residents of multi-unit properties); a posting sign when a product has a restricted entry interval (`required = false`); a registry advance notice per match with `required = true` and `due_by = scheduled start`, because a registry match blocks until notice is recorded. The home page labels `required = false` rows "best practice, no state rule on file".
8. Channels: email or SMS only when the pack sets `notice.electronicNeedsConsent = false` or the contact's `e_notice_consent_at` is on file at creation time; otherwise `print` (mail when the recipient has only a postal address and `notice.*.channels` allows mail). Without a pack, electronic delivery needs consent (conservative). Email and SMS rows stay `due` until spec 11 sends them; the office may print any notice and record it as printed, which changes the channel to `print` and logs the change. Proof is immutable once `sent`.
9. Registries: an import replaces the rows of one (state, kind, year) in full, keyed by row hash; matching sets `properties.registry_matches` for every property in that state by normalized address and clears matches that no longer exist; saving a property re-matches it (spec 02's property save calls `matchProperty`). The dispatch block lifts only when a `registry_advance` notice for that visit and that `for_scheduled_for` is `sent`; moving the visit to another date needs a new notice.
10. Alerts: credentials (`cert_expires_on`, `license_expires_on`) and branch licenses raise a `credential_expiring` or `license_expiring` task when 60 days and again when 30 days remain, and when already expired; tasks are unique per subject and due date, so the job never duplicates them; saving a later expiry closes the open task. Dismissing a task never changes dispatch: expiry still blocks (spec 05). The 60 and 30 day lead times are product policy, not state rules.
11. Permissions: `compliance.read` reads everything here; `dispatch.write` (dispatchers) and `agreements.read` (the office) read records and notices; a technician, who holds `dispatch.read` for the route but not `dispatch.write`, reads records of visits they were on the crew of and nothing else; `compliance.write` writes amendments, waivers, registries, licenses, tasks and the export; `dispatch.write` may record registry advance notices and print proof so the dispatcher can clear a block; nobody updates a locked record or an amendment. Registries, records and notices never cross tenants.
12. Times: due times, lock times and business days are computed in the tenant's time zone and stored in UTC; business days skip Saturday and Sunday (holidays are a pack question).
13. Export: `from` and `to` are business dates; the zip holds every record with a business date in range (locked and draft, marked), its product lines, amendments, every notice created in range with proof, every credential and branch license current at any point in range, and a binder HTML that prints as one document; the export writes an audit row with the range and who ran it. The CSV columns are fixed and documented in the binder; a pack's `report.format` adds a state file later.

## Edge cases
- A visit is completed, then a photo or note arrives from the device after lock: the capture is saved on the visit, the record keeps its locked snapshot, and the record page shows "captured after lock" for the difference.
- A pesticide visit completed with no product lines (only possible by import): record created incomplete with a task; the job never invents an application.
- The property's address or state is edited after the record: the record keeps the address and state at application time.
- A contact revokes consent (spec 02 clears `e_notice_consent_at`): new notices go to paper; sent ones keep their proof.
- Registry re-import drops an address: the property's match is cleared, the open `registry_notice_due` task closes, and a still-due advance notice to the dropped entry is waived by the job with the reason "registry entry removed on re-import"; a sent advance notice stays on file.
- Daylight saving day: end of day and hours-before computations use the tenant's time zone (tested on a spring-forward and a fall-back date).
- Two tenants import the same state registry: each has its own rows; matches never cross tenants.
- The job runs twice in the same minute: counts of created rows are zero the second time.
- A record is locked while a technician's offline queue still holds a change for the visit: the change applies to the visit (captures are not refused) and the record page shows the difference; it never rewrites the snapshot.
- Export over a range with no records: the zip still has the headers and the binder says so.

## Decisions
- Records are snapshots, not views: an inspector must see what was true at the time, so the record copies the visit, captures and credentials at lock and never recomputes.
- Lock timing and retention come only from the pack; without one the lock is the end of the business day and retention is open ended, both conservative readings.
- Notices without a pack are created as "best practice" rows rather than invented legal duties, except registry advance notices, which block scheduling and are therefore always required once an address matches.
- Electronic delivery is left to spec 11; this spec owns the obligation, the content and the proof. Print is always available so no required notice is stuck behind a missing integration.
- The compliance job is an HTTP route plus a script rather than a database scheduler, so it runs the same on Vercel cron and locally.
- Zip through `fflate` (small, pure JavaScript) so the export is one file as the discovery doc asked.
- Branch licenses are a warning until a pack confirms the state requires a license per location, except an expired license on file, which blocks: the conservative default would stop every demo branch without a license, and an expired license is a known fact.

## Open questions
- Registry file formats and registry kinds per launch state (pack `registry.fileFormat`, `registry.kinds`). The header-mapped CSV importer applies until then.
- Whether an e-signed consent counts as written consent for electronic notices in each launch state (PRD decision 9).
- State report formats (`report.format`); the generic bundle applies until then.
- Resident notices in a building with no resident contacts on file: a posting sign is the default; a state may require door-to-door delivery.
