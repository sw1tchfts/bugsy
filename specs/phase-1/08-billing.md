# Spec 08: Billing: Stripe Connect, invoices, autopay, sales tax

Status: done
Depends on: 04, 06
Skills to load: pest-accounting, pest-domain

## Goal
The agreement's billing mode turns into invoices without anyone typing them: per visit when the stop completes, monthly installments on their dates, one prepayment at the start of the term. Each line carries the service type that decides its sales tax for the property's state and address. Customers pay by card or bank account saved on file with autopay, by card charged from the office, or by cash and check recorded at the stop or in the office; every payment is allocated to invoices and the account's statement shows the whole position across its agreements. Past-due accounts are flagged, retried, optionally charged a late fee, and can hold service. Cancellations settle with a final invoice or a credit, as the agreement's own numbers say. Money is integer cents everywhere, and the ledger (09) reads these tables rather than being told about them.

## Users and stories
- As office staff, I see an invoice appear when a per-visit stop completes, the month's installment invoices on their dates, and the prepay invoice at signing, each with the right tax, and I never type one by hand except for a one-off charge.
- As office staff, I record a check or cash payment against an account and it lands on the oldest open invoice, or on the invoice I pick.
- As office staff, I charge a saved card for an open invoice, or add a card or bank account for a customer who asked for autopay, and I see what each attempt did.
- As office staff, I print or save an invoice and an account statement for a customer who wants paper, and I put the customer's PO number on invoices that need one.
- As an owner, I switch on late fees and the past-due hold, set the grace period, and connect our Stripe account so money goes to us.
- As a dispatcher, I see on the visit that the account is past due, and the hold stops me scheduling when the owner chose that.
- As a technician, I take cash or a check at the stop and record it with the visit.
- As a bookkeeper, I see tax collected by jurisdiction for the filing period, and the ledger (09) finds every invoice, payment and refund it needs here.
- As a customer (16), my invoice shows what was done, what was taxed and what I owe; autopay charges what I agreed to and nothing else.

## Scope
### In scope
- Tables: invoices, invoice_lines, payments, payment_allocations, payment_methods, autopay_attempts, billing_settings; the shared tax_rates table; `past_due_since`, tax exemption and gateway customer columns on accounts; the `invoice` counter.
- Invoice generation per billing mode from spec 04's `billingSchedule`, idempotent by source key; the per-visit invoice born in the completion transaction; a billing job (`POST /api/billing/run`, `npm run billing:run`, the office button) for installments, prepayments, safety-net visit invoices, autopay charges and retries, past-due flags and late fees.
- Sales tax by the service type's tax class for the property's state and the rates in force for the service address, exemption certificates, per-line rounding, the tax collected report.
- Payment gateway adapter with two implementations behind one interface: Stripe Connect (Standard accounts, direct charges, platform fee built and set to zero) when keys exist, and a local gateway that behaves like Stripe without the network for the local MVP and the tests. Card data never reaches our database in either.
- Saved payment methods with autopay, office charges, cash and check payments, refunds to the original card or a credit memo, allocations and unallocated credit.
- Collections: due dates from the account's terms, grace days, past-due flag on the account, autopay retries, optional late fees, the dispatch warning or hold.
- Cancellation settlement: the final invoice or credit memo and refund that spec 04's preview promised.
- PO numbers and net terms; void with a reason; manual one-off invoices; printable invoice and statement pages; the technician app's cash and check capture on per-visit stops.
- Office pages under `/billing`; a billing summary on the customer account page.

### Out of scope
- Sending invoices, receipts and reminders by email or text (11); this spec renders them and marks what is due to go.
- Journal entries, the contract balance subledger, period close and financial statements (09). 09 posts from invoices, invoice_lines, payments and payment_allocations.
- Card readers and tap to pay at the stop (the native wrapper, spec 06 packaging note); the stop records cash and check here.
- The customer portal (16): paying online, updating a card, viewing history.
- Platform billing (tenants paying for the software): a separate module later (13), never mixed with tenant billing.
- Loading real state tax rate tables: rates are data in `tax_rates` with a source and effective dates, loaded by the platform when a launch state is chosen; none is loaded here and no rate is invented.
- Commissions, inventory cost and job costing (17, 18).

## Business rules
1. Invoice numbers come from the tenant's `invoice` counter when the invoice is issued, never on draft, so issued numbers are gapless per tenant; an issued invoice's number, lines, totals, source and dates never change (trigger). Corrections are a void with a reason plus a new invoice, or a credit memo. (pest-accounting: every entry traceable)
2. Per-visit billing: a completed visit with a price above zero produces its invoice in the completion transaction, issued on the business date of departure, with one line for the visit (service type, price, tax). A re-service or any $0 visit produces no invoice. The billing job creates the invoice later if a completion somehow lacks one (`source_key = visit:<id>`). (fixtures: per_visit position is always zero)
3. Flat monthly billing: the schedule from spec 04 (`term_months` times renewals installments, floor rounding, remainder last) becomes one invoice per installment, issued by the job on the installment date in the tenant's time zone, never earlier, with `source_key = installment:<agreement>:<n>`. A cancelled or ended agreement issues no further installments. (fixtures/accounting/agreement-ledger.json)
4. Annual prepay: one invoice for the contract total on `starts_on` (or at signing when later), `source_key = prepay:<agreement>`. Renewal issues the next term's prepayment on the renewal's start.
5. Tax: a line's tax class is the service type's `tax_class` for the property's state (spec 03): `taxable` applies the rates in force for the property's state and postal code on the issue date (sum of `tax_rates` rows, each kept in the breakdown), `exempt` applies none, `unknown` applies none and marks the invoice `tax_unconfirmed` so the office sees it. An account with an unexpired exemption certificate applies none (`exempt_certificate`). Tax is computed per line and rounded half up to the cent; the invoice total is the sum of lines plus their tax; mixed invoices show taxable and nontaxable portions. No rate is hardcoded anywhere; a state with no rows taxes nothing and says so. (pest-accounting: sales tax; posting-rules fixture: 6000 at 0.055 taxes 330)
6. Terms: `due_on = issued_on + account.terms_days` (0 means due on issue). When the account requires a PO, an invoice stays draft until a PO number is set, and the job reports it on the billing home. The PO number prints on the invoice.
7. Payments: a cash, check or other payment is recorded with the day it was received and allocated to the account's open invoices oldest due first, or to the one invoice chosen; an amount beyond what is open stays unallocated as account credit and the job allocates it to the next issued invoice. Allocations never exceed the payment or the invoice (trigger). A refund is its own `refund` row pointing at the payment it reverses, sent to the gateway when that payment was a card or bank charge.
8. Autopay: when an issued invoice has an amount open and the account has an active default method with autopay on, the job charges it on `due_on` (on issue when terms are 0), one attempt row per try, with an idempotency key of the attempt. A failed charge is retried per `autopay_retry_days` after the failure, at most one try per plan date, and after the last failure the invoice stays open and the account is flagged. Nothing is ever charged twice for the same invoice and attempt. (PRD: automatic retries)
9. Invoice status: `draft` until issued; `issued` with nothing paid; `partially_paid` with some; `paid` when `paid_cents = total_cents`; `past_due` when open after `due_on + grace_days` (set by the job and by the allocation trigger); `void` by hand with a reason only while nothing is allocated. Credit memos are `issued` and count against the balance; they never become past due.
10. Past due: `accounts.past_due_since` is the oldest past-due invoice's due date and clears when none is past due. Dispatch shows a warning on every visit of a past-due account, and refuses to schedule when `pause_when_past_due` is on (an already scheduled visit is left to the dispatcher). (PRD: past-due flag that can pause service)
11. Late fees: when a late fee is configured (fixed cents, percent of the open balance, or both), the job issues one `late_fee` invoice per past-due invoice the first time it is past due (`source_key = late_fee:<invoice>`), never again for the same invoice, with a nontaxable line. The fee is a separate invoice so the original stays as issued.
12. Cancellation: when spec 04 cancels an agreement, `settleCancellation` turns the preview into money: delivered but unbilled service becomes a final invoice (source `settlement`) with the cancellation fee as its own line; prepaid but undelivered service becomes a credit memo, refunded to the original card or bank charge when the money came that way, else left as credit on the account. The contract position is zero afterwards. (fixture E: recognized 30000, billed 22500, final invoice 7500; a 5000 fee is its own line)
13. Gateway: one connected account per tenant (`billing_settings.gateway_account_id`); charges are direct charges on the connected account with a platform fee of zero until the platform sets `PLATFORM_FEE_BPS`; card and bank details are tokenized by the gateway and only brand, last four and expiry are stored; webhooks are recorded once by event id and applied idempotently. The local gateway follows the same interface so the product works end to end with no keys.
14. Money: every amount column is `bigint` cents; tax rates are `numeric`; the only arithmetic on rates happens in `taxForLine` with integer cents out. Statements are the sum of issued invoices and credit memos minus succeeded payments plus refunds, with a running balance per line.
15. Permissions: `billing.read` sees invoices, payments, methods (brand and last four) and settings; `billing.write` issues, voids, records payments, charges, manages methods and settings; `visits.write` (the technician) records a cash or check payment for a visit on their crew, allocated only to that visit's invoice; the owner's `*` covers the gateway connection. Customers' own access arrives with the portal (16).

## Edge cases
- A visit completes before the agreement's prepay or installment is issued (a technician out early): the visit invoice is not created for flat monthly or prepay agreements; only per-visit agreements bill at completion.
- Installment date falls on the 31st in a short month: spec 04's schedule clamps the date; the job issues on that date.
- The job runs late (a weekend of downtime): every installment whose date has passed is issued with its own date as `issued_on`, so due dates and aging stay true.
- A property changes state after an invoice: the invoice keeps the tax it computed; the next one uses the new address.
- A tax rate ends mid-term: the rate in force on each invoice's issue date applies; the breakdown names the rows.
- A customer pays twice by mistake: the second payment is unallocated credit; the office refunds it or it covers the next invoice.
- Autopay with a removed method: the attempt is `skipped` with the reason and the account's past-due path proceeds.
- Charging a voided or paid invoice: refused before the gateway is called.
- A refund larger than the payment: refused.
- The technician records cash for a visit whose invoice is not issued yet (the completion transaction creates it, so this cannot happen for per-visit; for flat monthly stops the payment is unallocated credit on the account).
- Stripe webhook for a tenant whose connected account is unknown: stored in `gateway_events` and ignored with a log line.
- Void after a partial payment: refused; refund the payment first, then void.

## Decisions
- Standard connected accounts with direct charges: the tenant owns the Stripe relationship, disputes and payouts, which fits a platform whose customers are independent companies; the platform fee is built in and zero (PRD answer).
- A local gateway with the same interface, rather than mocking Stripe in tests: the MVP runs with no keys, the flows are exercised end to end, and the switch to Stripe is configuration plus onboarding.
- Invoice numbers per tenant, not per branch (PRD answer), assigned at issue so drafts never consume numbers.
- Taxability unknown taxes nothing and flags the invoice, the conservative default the PRD chose (do not tax), rather than blocking billing.
- Tax rates keyed by postal code: good enough for state plus county and city tables, and a state pack can add a finer lookup later without changing the invoice line shape.
- Late fees as their own invoices and refunds as their own payment rows: issued documents never change, which is what the ledger and an auditor need.
- Cash and check at the stop ride the existing sync queue as one more change kind; card at the door waits for the wrapper's reader.
- Reminders and receipts are not sent here: spec 11 owns every message, and this spec leaves the facts (due dates, past due, receipts) for it to read.

## Open questions
- Each launch state's taxability of structural pest control and of yard mosquito and tick spraying, and its rate tables and sources (`tax_rates` stays empty until then).
- Whether ACH debits need a separate mandate text per state for autopay; the gateway stores the mandate acceptance Stripe returns.
- Whether a dispute (chargeback) should reopen the invoice automatically; for now the webhook records it and the office reverses by hand.
