# Market scan: competitors outside Bugsy's set of 8, and 2025 to 2026 market dynamics

Researched 2026-10-29 from public pages only. Scope: pest control features only. The eight competitors already in the set (PestPac, FieldRoutes, ServSuite, Briostack, Pocomos, GorillaDesk, Jobber, Housecall Pro) appear here only in market news such as acquisitions and AI launches. Items dated before 2025 are labeled "(older)". Most G2, Capterra and GetApp category pages returned HTTP 403 when fetched, so category-wide counts come from search snippets and are marked that way. Many sources are vendor marketing or competitor blogs, and each one is labeled as such.

## 1. Which pest-specific products exist beyond the eight, and how do the important ones compare?

### Takeaway
About ten more pest-specific products are live. Fieldwork is the closest match to Bugsy's commercial pitch: it publishes prices per field user, office users are free, it costs $59 to $99, and it produces California and New York state usage logs. PestBase.ai lists the feature set closest to Bugsy's compliance list. None of these products names Wisconsin, and none has a built-in general ledger. SavvyPest and PestScout could not be found.

### Cited Findings
**Category size**
- G2 tracks 46 products in its pest control category, with stats updated May 29, 2026. This comes from a search snippet; the page returned 403 when fetched. — [G2 pest control category](https://www.g2.com/categories/pest-control)

**Fieldwork (fieldworkhq.com)**
- Pricing for 1 field user: Startup $59/mo, Business $79/mo (adds Google mapping, routing optimization, device management), Pro $99/mo (adds contracts and agreements, technician tracking, multi-office, Zapier, API). — [Fieldwork pricing](https://fieldworkhq.com/pricing/)
- "Office staff members are free. We only charge for users who are out in the field." QuickBooks integration, customer portal, reminders and reporting come with every plan. — [Fieldwork pricing](https://fieldworkhq.com/pricing/)

- "If you live in California or New York Fieldwork can generate the state required pesticide usage logs."

  — [Fieldwork chemical tracking](https://fieldworkhq.com/chemical-tracking-and-reporting/)

**SavvyPest and PestScout:** searches returned nothing under either name.

### Inferences
- Fieldwork is the most direct challenge to Bugsy's pricing pitch.
  - Model: it charges per field user and office users are free.
  - Price: $59 to $99, overlapping Bugsy's $79 to $99 hypothesis.
  - It already ships state-format usage reports, though only for CA and NY.
  - Its gaps against Bugsy: Wisconsin, notices, license tracking, a general ledger, and possibly offline.
- PestBase's feature list reads almost like Bugsy's compliance list (EPA number, SDS, certification matching, state reporting, offline, no setup fee). Its owner, customers and launch date are unknown, so it may be early stage. It is worth watching.
- Smart Service is the opposite bet to Bugsy: compliance capture with QuickBooks as the ledger. Owners whose accountant insists on QuickBooks may prefer it.
- Capturing an EPA registration number per application is now common in pest-specific tools, including cheap ones. That narrows the PRD's CMP-02 wedge ("0 of 8"). The distinctions that hold up are a curated master catalog with label and SDS, state-specific record completeness, and notices.

### Gaps
- No Wisconsin or Midwest customers were found for any of these products, and no vendor page names Wisconsin.
- Offline capability could not be confirmed for Fieldwork, PestBoss, Smart Service, Service Autopilot or WorkWave Service.
- The owners and funding of PestBase and PestBoss are unverified. PestBoss's founding year conflicts between sources (2010 vs 2021).
- WorkWave Service's pest features and real pricing could not be found from public pages.
- Review counts and ratings from G2 and Capterra could not be pulled directly (HTTP 403).

## 2. Which horizontal field-service tools do pest companies use, and how deep is their pest support?

### Takeaway
Horizontal tools now commonly offer chemical logging with EPA numbers, through custom fields or a small structured record. None generates state reports, and none sends regulatory customer notices or tracks applicator licenses. ServiceTitan itself sends smaller pest firms to FieldRoutes.

### Cited Findings
**ServiceTitan**
- The pest page targets multi-truck to multi-branch operations. Its FAQ says: "Smaller pest control businesses with less complex needs are typically a better fit for FieldRoutes."
- "Log chemicals in the Field App with compliance fields." The page mentions EPA registrations.
- It sells 24/7 voice agents.
- "ServiceTitan's pricing is per-technician." The price is not published.
- The page does not mention license tracking, state reports or notices.
  — [ServiceTitan pest page](https://www.servicetitan.com/industries/pest-control-software)

**Arrivy**

- A listing shows a starting price of about $50 per user, which may be out of date. — [Software Advice AU](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/520653/Arrivy-Field-Service-Management)

**Kickserv**

**Service Fusion**

### Inferences
- The PRD's line that horizontal tools have "no device programs, labels, safety data sheets or state reports" still holds for state reports, labels and SDS. Basic chemical capture with an EPA number is no longer a differentiator against horizontal tools.
- ServiceTitan's routing of small firms to FieldRoutes means the ServiceTitan group meets Bugsy's 3 to 15 technician target through FieldRoutes, not ServiceTitan core.

### Gaps
- Workiz: no official pest or chemical documentation found; its guessed pest URL returned 404.

- Reddit r/pestcontrol threads did not surface in search, so no first-hand operator software opinions are included.

- FieldRoutes' planned churn indicators would remove RPT-02 (retention analytics, "0 of 8" in the PRD) from the wedge once shipped. Its 2025 offline improvements weaken the PRD's technician offline pain point for FieldRoutes specifically. The PRD's offline evidence names Housecall Pro and GorillaDesk.
- AI routing (WAIve overnight optimization, FieldRoutes autoscheduling) is now an expectation at the mid-market. Bugsy's decision to leave route optimization out of Phase 1 fits the 3 to 15 technician target, but it will be compared against these.

### Gaps
- Pricing for WAIve and for FieldRoutes' AI features is not public.
- No independent evidence was found on how well AI receptionists book pest jobs.
- Scout AI's and Driven's current status and customers are unverified.

## 4. Are there compliance-only tools for pesticide recordkeeping, state reporting or notices, and does any support Wisconsin ATCP 29 or the landscape registry?

### Takeaway
No commercial compliance-only tool for structural pest control was found, and no product of any kind names Wisconsin, ATCP 29 or the landscape registry. Existing recordkeeping tools are agricultural and mostly old. State reporting inside pest software covers only California, New York and Arizona.

### Cited Findings
**Recordkeeping tools (agricultural, mostly old)**
- Nebraska's free PeRK app is discontinued. — [UNL CropWatch](https://cropwatch.unl.edu/unl-releases-perk-20-pesticide-recordkeeping-app/)
- Frontier Precision's PAR system claimed it met record rules in 27 states (older, 2015). — [Frontier Precision](https://frontierprecision.com/news/pesky-paperwork-pesticides/)
- Fulcrum offers configurable pesticide-application form templates, aimed at agriculture. — [Fulcrum](https://fulcrumapp.com/apps/pesticide-application-apples)

**State reporting inside pest software**
- Fieldwork: CA and NY. — [Fieldwork](https://fieldworkhq.com/chemical-tracking-and-reporting/)
- PestPac: California school and agricultural commissioner reporting, per a vendor answer on G2. — [G2 discussion](https://www.g2.com/discussions/how-does-pestpac-handle-chemical-tracking-and-compliance-documentation-for-state-audits)
- PestBase claims "state pesticide use reporting" without naming states. — [PestBase](https://www.pestbase.ai/)
- FieldCamp explicitly does not file state reports. — [FieldCamp docs](https://docs.fieldcamp.ai/data-model/chemicals)

**Wisconsin**
- A search for pest software that supports Wisconsin ATCP 29 returned only the code itself (ATCP 29.21 business records, 29.51 advance notice, 29.55 residential structures). — [Wis. Legislature ATCP 29](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/code/admin_code/atcp/020/29/_45?up=1)
- Wisconsin's landscape registry has existed since 1993. Applicators must notify registrants at least 12 hours before treating; sign-up is through MyDATCP and closes February 1; the list is searchable for applicators from mid-March. No vendor tool for it was found. — [WPR](https://www.wpr.org/culture/pesticide-registry-deadline-fast-approaches); [DATCP registry document](https://datcp.wi.gov/Documents/LandscapeRegistry2020.pdf)

**Other states' registries**
- Colorado's Pesticide-Sensitive Registry moved to an online, searchable database in July 2024 and uses GIS to find properties within 250 feet. — [Colorado Dept. of Agriculture](https://ag.colorado.gov/pesticide-sensitive-registry-psr)
- No commercial registry-notice software was found for any state.

### Inferences
- The PRD's wedge features CMP-05 (automatic notices) and CMP-08 (Wisconsin compliance) are confirmed as open: nobody automates registry or post-application notices.
- State reporting exists elsewhere, so a competitor could add Wisconsin if the market were worth it. Fieldwork already does per-state logs. The moat is speed and depth on Wisconsin, not technical difficulty.

### Gaps
- DATCP's current registry page returned 404, so the registry's 2026 distribution format could not be verified from a primary page. A search summary of DATCP materials said a sortable spreadsheet, PDF and interactive map, but this is unverified. This bears on the PRD's open DATCP question.
- A search summary also reported Pennsylvania's registry rules (a 500-foot radius and 12 to 72 hours' notice), but no source page was opened, so this is unverified.

## 5. What happened in the market in 2025 to 2026: software M&A, operator consolidation, pricing?

No pest-software acquisition was found in 2025 or 2026; ownership is unchanged since 2021 or 2022. Operators keep consolidating, including in Wisconsin: Greenix, a PE-backed platform, bought Milwaukee and Madison operations in 2025. ## 6. Does any competitor offer a built-in double-entry general ledger or deferred revenue for service agreements?

### Takeaway
No pest-specific product found has a built-in double-entry ledger or deferred revenue tied to visits. That supports the PRD's claim beyond the eight. Native ledgers exist only in adjacent trade software (alarm, HVAC) and in ERPs. Accountants' recommended answer is an FSM plus Sage Intacct or QuickBooks, not a pest product with its own ledger.

### Cited Findings
**Pest products: none has a ledger**
- ERP Scorecard (July 2026) on specialist pest platforms:
  - FieldRoutes: "No GL; financials export to accounting system" and "No GAAP deferred revenue schedules"
  - PestPac: "No GL; built-in AR and payments"
  - GorillaDesk, Briostack, Fieldwork and Pocomos: no GL, with a QuickBooks link
  - Sage Intacct is the native GL that holds "deferred revenue schedules for prepaid agreements" behind the FSM
  - QuickBooks hits a "ceiling" with prepaid annual agreements at volume
  — [ERP Scorecard](https://erpscorecard.com/erp-systems/industries/field-services/pest-control)
- PestPac's own page says many smaller companies find its "built-in financial reports replace the need for QuickBooks". This is reporting, not a stated double-entry ledger. A third-party listicle claims PestPac has a built-in GL, which conflicts with the scorecard. — [PestPac accounting page](https://www.pestpac.com/features/accounting-software)
- Briostack says invoicing and accounting are "built into" its FSM. The scorecard lists no GL. — [Briostack accounting page](https://www.briostack.com/pest-control-software/invoicing-accounting-software)
- Smart Service relies on QuickBooks by design ("a QuickBooks add-on"). — [Smart Service](https://www.smartservice.com/pest-control-apps)
- PestBoss and PestBase list no accounting module. — [SourceForge PestBoss](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/product/PESTBOSS); [PestBase](https://www.pestbase.ai/)

**Adjacent trades with a native ledger**
- FieldHub (alarm and security, not pest) claims a native GL on the same database as dispatch and recurring billing, with revenue recognition and deferred revenue for recurring monitoring contracts. — [FieldHub](https://fieldhub.com/)
- Aptora 360: "True double-entry, full accrual foundation" for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and similar trades. It does not mention pest control or deferred revenue. — [Aptora](https://www.aptora.com/features/general-ledger)

**ERPs and resellers**
- IFS (enterprise) lists built-in accounting. Field-service ERPs such as Acumatica and Dynamics 365 tie service contracts to the GL. Standalone home-services FSM apps sync to external accounting. — [Capterra IFS](https://capterra.com/p/116489/IFS/); [ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/en-us/field-service-erp)
- A Sage Intacct reseller markets add-ons for pest control and home services. — [SJ Rollins Sage Intacct](https://sageintacct.sjrollins.com/home-services)

**Accounting practice**
- Pest-industry accountants advise tying deferred revenue on annual agreements to completed visits. — [TBA & Associates](https://tbaandassociates.com/industries/pest-control/)

### Inferences
- The ACC-01 and ACC-04 wedge holds across everything found. The risk is less a competitor than buyer indifference: outside accountants and the market default (FSM plus QuickBooks, Intacct for larger firms) may resist a second ledger. The PRD's summarized journal export for QuickBooks and the accountant sign-off at the pilot gate address this directly.
- FieldHub and Aptora show that FSM plus native GL works in other recurring-service trades (alarm monitoring has the same prepaid-contract shape). A well-funded horizontal player could copy the idea, but none has done so for pest control.

### Gaps
- FieldHub's and Aptora's claims are vendor marketing and were not tested. Whether either has pest customers is unknown.
- Whether Wintac or ServiceLedger have a native GL and pest customers was not verified; the Wintac search returned only a 2007 item.

## 7. What confirms or undercuts Bugsy's three-part wedge, and how threatening is each competitor?

### Takeaway
Wedge (2), the general ledger with deferred revenue, is fully confirmed. Wedge (1), compliance built into the visit, is confirmed for Wisconsin rules, notices and license tracking, but basic EPA-number chemical capture is now common, even in horizontal tools. Wedge (3), transparent all-inclusive pricing, is only partly differentiating: Fieldwork, GorillaDesk, Service Autopilot, QuoteIQ and FieldCamp publish prices, and Fieldwork uses nearly the same per-field-user, office-free model at $59 to $99.

### Cited Findings
**Wedge 1: compliance in the visit**
- Confirmed: no vendor names Wisconsin. State reports exist only for CA and NY (Fieldwork) and CA (PestPac). No automated registry or post-application notices were found. — [Fieldwork](https://fieldworkhq.com/chemical-tracking-and-reporting/); [G2 PestPac answer](https://www.g2.com/discussions/how-does-pestpac-handle-chemical-tracking-and-compliance-documentation-for-state-audits); [ATCP 29 search result](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/code/admin_code/atcp/020/29/_45?up=1)

**Wedge 2: general ledger and deferred revenue**

**Wedge 3: pricing**

**Roadmap pressure on other PRD wedge items**
- FieldRoutes' planned churn indicators target RPT-02. — [Finviz press release](https://finviz.com/news/400218/fieldroutes-to-unveil-next-phase-of-product-innovation-at-ignite-2026)

### Inferences
- The positioning line should lead with "Wisconsin-complete records and notices, plus correct books". "We track EPA numbers" and "we publish prices" are no longer unique.
- Price against Fieldwork, not only against GorillaDesk and Briostack. At $79 to $99 per technician, Bugsy has to justify the gap to Fieldwork's $59 Startup tier with the general ledger, notices and Wisconsin rules.
- Add an API or webhooks (PLT-03) to the roadmap so AI receptionist and call-tracking vendors can connect. Many of them now advertise FieldRoutes, PestPac and GorillaDesk connections.
- Retention analytics (RPT-02) should be treated as a time-limited advantage: FieldRoutes has previewed churn indicators but given no ship date.

### Gaps
- No public evidence shows how Wisconsin operators weigh ledger, compliance and price against each other. Only the pilot and interviews can answer that.
- No Wisconsin customer of any competitor outside the set was identified.
